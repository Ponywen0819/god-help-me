\begin{abstract}

表皮內神經纖維密度 (Intraepidermal Nerve Fiber Density, IENFD) 是診斷小纖維神經病變 (Small Fiber Neuropathy) 的關鍵臨床指標。目前臨床上仍以病理切片影像之人工計數為主，除耗時費力外，計數結果亦高度依賴判讀者的主觀經驗。近年雖有以 U-Net 為代表的深度學習模型嘗試自動化此流程，然而皮膚切片染色呈現的神經纖維常因染色不均、訊雜比低、以及纖維橫跨表皮與真皮時發生之斷裂，導致語意分割結果呈現破碎化，同一條纖維被拆解為多個不連通之元件，無法直接轉換為可計數的纖維拓樸結構。

本研究提出 \textbf{標註擴張連結演算法}（Annotation-Grow Linker, 以下簡稱 AGL），將此「片段重組」問題建模為基於像素成本圖 (cost map) 的多源最短路徑擴張與圖論問題。演算法首先以綠色通道影像經形態學背景消除、對比受限直方圖均衡化 (CLAHE) 與 Sato 血管性濾波 (vesselness filter) 建構像素成本圖；接著以每個標註連通元件為種子，執行多源 Dijkstra 擴張，使每個像素記錄其所屬元件與最短成本；再自擴張場中搜尋相鄰元件間之最小成本相遇點以構建元件層圖，並以成本閾值裁剪不合理之連線後求最小生成森林 (Minimum Spanning Forest)；最後將回溯路徑與原標註合併、進行骨架化以得到像素級拓樸圖，並依表皮／真皮區域標記統計有效的神經纖維跨越數。

本方法在保留原標註可靠區段的同時，能透過影像成本圖所導引之最短路徑補足斷裂區段，避免了以距離為唯一準則時所產生之非生物合理連線。在內部資料集之實驗顯示，相較於既有的 DBSCAN 連結法與純粹最小生成樹連結法，本方法於有效跨越數之計算上能有效逼近人工標註結果，並在纖維拓樸之連續性與可解釋性上取得良好平衡。

\textbf{關鍵字：} 表皮內神經纖維密度、小纖維神經病變、神經纖維重建、多源最短路徑、最小生成樹、血管性濾波、骨架化。

\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract*}

Intraepidermal Nerve Fiber Density (IENFD) is a key clinical marker for the diagnosis of small fiber neuropathy. In current practice the measurement still relies on manual counting on histological sections, which is labor intensive and subjective. Recent attempts based on deep semantic segmentation models such as U-Net can highlight nerve fibers on the section, yet uneven staining, low signal-to-noise ratio, and fiber interruption across the epidermal/dermal boundary frequently produce fragmented segmentation masks in which a single fiber is split into multiple disconnected components. A downstream topology that is suitable for counting cannot therefore be obtained by skeletonizing the raw segmentation.

This thesis proposes \textbf{Annotation-Grow Linker (AGL)}, which formulates the fragment-reassembly task as a multi-source shortest-path expansion on a pixel-level cost map combined with a graph-theoretic reconstruction. The pipeline first builds an enhanced image from the green channel using morphological background removal, Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE), and the Sato vesselness filter, and converts it into a traversal cost map. Each connected annotation component is then used as a seed of a multi-source Dijkstra expansion so that every reachable pixel records its owning component and the minimum accumulated cost. Meeting points between adjacent components are searched on the resulting field to build a component-level graph, whose edges are pruned by a cost threshold before a minimum spanning forest is computed. Finally, the back-tracked MST paths are merged with the original annotation and skeletonized to yield a pixel-level topology graph, from which effective nerve fiber crossings across the epidermal/dermal boundary are counted.

The proposed method preserves the reliable portions of the original annotation while completing the broken fragments through image-guided shortest paths, avoiding the non-biologically plausible links produced by purely distance-based linkers. On our internal dataset the method closes the gap between automated and expert counts of effective crossings compared with a DBSCAN-based linker and a pure minimum spanning tree linker, while offering better interpretability of the reconstructed topology.

\textbf{Keywords:} Intraepidermal Nerve Fiber Density, Small Fiber Neuropathy, Neural Fiber Reconstruction, Multi-source Dijkstra, Minimum Spanning Tree, Vesselness Filter, Skeletonization.

\end{abstract*}
